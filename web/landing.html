Security & Privacy

Your privacy is our priority. Here's how we protect you.

🔐 Anonymous Authentication

No email, no phone number, no personal data. You get a unique ID that's only linked to your device. Complete anonymity from day one.

🛡️ End-to-End Security

Your notes are stored securely in Firebase. All data is encrypted and only accessible by you. We never track, sell, or share your data.

📌 PIN Lock & Hidden Notes

Lock the entire app with a PIN and hide sensitive notes from the main list. Multiple layers of protection so your private thoughts stay private.

💣 Self-Destructing Notes

Send notes that delete themselves automatically after being read or after a set time. Share confidential info with zero trace.

⚡ No Ads. No Trackers. Ever.

Minimal data collection. No advertising. No third-party analytics. Pure, simple, private note-taking — the way it should be.
