📖 About

🔐 Privacy First

No traditional sign-up required. AnonNote uses anonymous authentication to create a unique user ID. Your notes are securely stored and only accessible by you. We believe privacy is a fundamental right, not a premium feature.

⚙️ How It Works

Instant & Effortless: When you open the app, you are automatically signed in anonymously. Your notes are stored securely in the cloud and linked only to your device. Create, edit, and search notes instantly without any friction.
